# Research Hypothesis

The current CleanArchitectureTemplate V2 works, but its instruction architecture
has limited modularity and hierarchy.

The V3 experiment will investigate whether applying software architecture
principles to Claude Code instructions can improve:

- modularity;
- separation of concerns;
- single source of truth;
- instruction loading and triggering;
- architectural decision control;
- maintainability;
- Claude autonomy.

The resulting V3 should preserve useful behavior of V2 while reducing
duplication, architectural violations, and required human intervention.
